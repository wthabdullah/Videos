Step 2 · Sort
2
Step 2 of 3

Sort and use.

Sorting keeps waste worth something. Mixed, it is worth nothing.

Three colours: green, blue, red.No coloured bins? Three bags or boxes will do. Use the same colours at home and on your street.
GREEN

Organic

Peels, food scraps, leaves, garden waste.
Make compostMix with dry leaves or torn paper. Keep it damp, turn it now and then, and wait.
BLUE

Recyclable

Paper, cardboard, cans, glass, plastic bottles and tubs.
Sell itKeep it clean and dry. Sell it to a scrap dealer or take it to a collection point.
RED

Special

Batteries, electronics, chemicals, medicines, sharp things, sanitary waste.
Keep it apartIn its own bag, wrapped. Ask your local authority where to hand it in.

Respect the people who handle our waste

Wrap broken glass and needles. Sort properly. Treat waste workers with dignity.

The 8-week action plan.

A home that sorts has a habit. A street that sorts can ask for sorted collection.

Weeks 1–2

Form a team. Map who already collects waste, who cleans up, and who could help.

2

Show

Weeks 3–4

With permission, put out model bins in the three colours. Sort a heap of mixed waste in public, and explain why sorting is everyone’s responsibility, morally and legally.

3

Coordinate

Weeks 5–6

Meet the local authority, collectors, shops, schools and scrap dealers. Agree who keeps each area clean.

4

Advocate

Weeks 7–8

Ask collectors to keep sorted waste sorted. Ask the municipality, or its contractor, to take charge of collection.

Your result: compare week 1 with week 8. The difference is what your street changed.

6WASTE TO WEALTH
The law
The law

What the law says.

Each province and region has its own law. These are the most a court can order, not what everyone pays.

Islamabad
Pakistan Environmental Protection Act 1997; Single-Use Plastics Regulations 2023
Fine up toRs 1 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
Punjab
Punjab Environmental Protection Act 1997, amended 2012
Fine up toRs 5 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
Sindh
Sindh Environmental Protection Act 2014
Fine up toRs 5 million
Repeat offence: up to 3 years in prison
Khyber Pakhtunkhwa
KP Environmental Protection Act 2014; Amendment Act 2022
FineRs 50,000 to Rs 5 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
Balochistan
Balochistan Environmental Protection Act 2012
Fine up toRs 1 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
Gilgit-Baltistan
Gilgit-Baltistan Environmental Protection Act 2014
Fine up toRs 1 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
Azad Jammu and Kashmir
AJK Environmental Protection Act 2000
Fine up toRs 1 million
Repeat offence: up to 2 years in prison
At home, the rule is simple: don’t burn waste, don’t dump it in drains or empty plots, and keep special waste apart.
